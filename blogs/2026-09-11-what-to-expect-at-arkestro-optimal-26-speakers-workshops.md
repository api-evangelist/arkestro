AI is changing what procurement and supply chain teams can accomplish, but the bigger question is how that intelligence gets applied to the decisions that affect enterprise performance. That’s the focus of Arkestro Optimal ’26, happening October 13th-15th in Houston. This year’s theme, Unlock the Predictive Advantage, will explore how organizations are using Predictive Procurement to expand their reach across enterprise spend and deliver measurable results faster.
